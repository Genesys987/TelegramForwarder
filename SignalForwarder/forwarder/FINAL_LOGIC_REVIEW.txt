**Issue**: No validation that TP levels are in correct order
**Fix**: Added warnings for incorrect TP ordering
**Logic**: 
- BUY signals: TPs should be ascending (higher than entry)
- SELL signals: TPs should be descending (lower than entry)
**Code Location**: `TelegramSignalForwarder.mq4` line 283

### ✅ 5. SL Position Validation (ADDED)
**Issue**: No validation that SL is on correct side of entry
**Fix**: Added warnings for incorrect SL placement
**Logic**:
- BUY signals: SL should be below entry price

**Code Location**: `TelegramSignalForwarder.mq4` line 321
